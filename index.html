About Bistro Noir Lahore

Where Architectural Noir Meets Culinary Artistry

Nestled on Lahore’s famed MM Alam Road in Gulberg III, Bistro Noir brings an alluring Parisian bistro aesthetic paired with authentic Italian gastronomic warmth. Our interior celebrates timeless noir minimalism — rich dark leather, warm amber lighting, brushed brass accents, and serene corners designed for intimate conversations and unforgettable dining memories.

Each dish is prepared with uncompromising passion: hand-stretched 12-inch brick-oven pizzas, melt-in-your-mouth slow-cooked beef, house-made pastas, and delicate desserts crafted fresh daily.

Wood-Fired Oven

Authentic Italian stone-baked sourdough pizzas with crispy blistered crusts.

Chef's Cuts

Hand-trimmed premium Angus beef, tenderloin filet mignon, and slow-cooked short ribs.

Private & VIP Dining

Dedicated lounge corners and intimate booth arrangements for private gatherings.

Valet & Convenience

Hassle-free parking and valet service on MM Alam Road, Gulberg III.
