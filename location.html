Transit & Driving Directions

How To Reach Us

Effortless access from every major neighborhood in Lahore.

From DHA Lahore (Phase 1–6)

Take Ring Road or Lahore Cantt via Cavalry Ground flyover into Main Boulevard Gulberg. Turn onto MM Alam Road at Hussain Chowk. Bistro Noir is located on the right side in Block B1. (Approx. 15–20 minutes drive).

From Mall Road & Cantt

Drive down Jail Road towards Main Boulevard Gulberg. Head south towards Mini Market and enter MM Alam Road. Valet attendants are stationed at the entrance for immediate assistance. (Approx. 10–12 minutes).

From Johar Town & Model Town

Proceed via Ferozepur Road or Kalma Chowk underpass directly into Main Boulevard Gulberg. Take the turn towards MM Alam Road. (Approx. 12–18 minutes drive).
